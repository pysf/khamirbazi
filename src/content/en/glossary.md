## Glossary

- **Baker's percentage:** every ingredient is written as a percentage of the flour weight. Flour is always 100%.
- **Tipo 00:** a very finely ground Italian wheat flour, often sold as pizza flour. The protein line on the pack shows the protein content.
- **Dry yeast:** instant yeast, commonly a 7 g packet. **Fresh yeast:** a soft cube, commonly 42 g. Check your pack.
- **Yeast solution:** yeast dissolved in a known amount of water, so you can measure a very small amount of yeast on a kitchen scale.
- **Baking paper:** parchment paper that you can use to slide the pizza into the oven. It is optional. Check its maximum temperature first. **Baking surface:** a pizza stone, a baking steel or a cast-iron pan that you heat in the oven. **Peel:** a flat paddle for sliding a pizza into the oven.
- **Top and bottom heat:** the oven mode that heats from above and below. **Fan mode:** the mode with a fan. **Grill:** strong heat from the top only.
- **Rise:** the dough gets bigger as the yeast makes gas. **Dough ball:** a portion of dough shaped into a ball, ready to rise and to be opened into a pizza. **Cold fermentation:** letting the dough rise slowly in the fridge.
- **Kneading:** working the dough by hand or machine to build gluten. **Autolyse:** a rest after you mix flour and water, before you knead.
- **Stretch and fold:** pull one side of the dough up and fold it over itself, turn the bowl, and repeat. It makes the dough stronger without hard kneading.
- **Windowpane test:** stretching a small piece of dough thin to see how strong the gluten is.
- **Browning:** the surface turning golden brown in the oven.
- **Semolina:** coarse durum wheat flour, used for dusting.
