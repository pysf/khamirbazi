Logo

The mark is a top-down pizza drawn with one ring, one cut-out slice and six dots: one per style. It is traced as clean SVG from the supplied concept. The name sits next to it in Inter 600 (English) or Vazirmatn 700 (Farsi). The mark has no left or right, so it works in both directions.

App icon and favicon: the mark simplified to the ring, the slice and two dots, so it still reads at 16 px. File: favicon.svg.

One-colour on red
  • Files in the repo: docs/design/logo/ — mark.svg (red), mark-ink.svg, mark-cream.svg, mark-on-dark.svg, favicon.svg.
  • Minimum size: 16 px for the favicon version, 24 px for the full mark. Clear space: the width of one ring thickness times three on every side.
  • Colours: tomato #B3321F on light, #FF8A70 on dark, charcoal #2B2A22 or cream #FBF6EC for one colour. No gradients, shadows or rotation.
