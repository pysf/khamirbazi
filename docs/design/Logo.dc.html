Logotype

A pure wordmark, no pictogram. The two dots of the Latin i become tomato-red circles (sauce, or a ball of dough seen from above). Set in Newsreader 600; the dotless ı is a normal character, so the logo is live text. The Farsi wordmark is Vazirmatn 700 with one matching tomato dot, because the Farsi letter dots cannot be coloured separately.
